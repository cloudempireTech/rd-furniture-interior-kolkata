3. Ergonomic Modular Kitchens: Marine-grade waterproof ply cabinets, acrylic/PU finishes, soft-close Blum/Hettich tandem boxes, spice pullouts, and durable granite/quartz countertops.

5. Handcrafted Solid Wood Dining & Seating: Teak wood 6-seater dining sets, artisanal diwans, sofa-cum-beds with pullout drawers, and solid garden/verandah benches.
6. Architectural Doors & Wood Panelling: Hand-carved spiritual and contemporary entrance doors, CNC brass inlay designs, and soundproof door frames.
