## Design System & Mandates
- **TheDivine Floating Pill Navbar:** Frosted glass header (`position: fixed; top: 14px;`) floating directly over the 16:9 hero video with zero white margin.
- **Strict Ban on Muddy Brown:** Built on a pristine porcelain background (`#fbfbfd`), crisp white surface cards (`#ffffff`), rich honey teak accents (`#d97706`), golden amber beacons (`#f59e0b`), and deep architectural slate typography (`#0f172a`).
- **Official WhatsApp SVG Contrast Standard:** High-contrast white bubble with green phone handset on green buttons (`#25D366`), and green bubble with white handset on dark/light surfaces. WhatsApp icon displayed next to every contact phone number.
- **Mobile Screen Boundary Guarantee:** Passes Playwright audit on 360x800 (Android baseline), 375x667 (iOS SE), and 390x844 (iOS 12/13/14) with strictly `scrollWidth === clientWidth`.
- **Pure Portfolio Standard:** Strictly zero e-commerce, zero carts, zero prices, and zero checkout flows.
