Our Workshop Story

Generational Bengali Woodcraft & Modern Precision

For over four decades, R D Furniture & Interior has been a hallmark of genuine timber integrity, uncompromising joinery, and personalized interior fittings in Thakurpukur, Kolkata.

Established in 1978

Rooted in Craft, Built for Lifetimes

Founded in 1978 near Brahmachari School on James Long Sarani, R D Furniture & Interior started as an authentic artisan carpentry atelier. Over 45 years, as Kolkata transitioned from ancestral homes to high-rise modern residences, our philosophy has stayed resolute: never compromise on wood seasoning, structural stability, and honest joinery.

Today, we blend traditional mortise-and-tenon craftsmanship with state-of-the-art modular fabrication. Every wardrobe, kitchen cabinet, and bed is built to resist Kolkata's humid monsoon cycles with marine-grade 710 boiling-water-proof cores and anti-termite wood treatment.

Material Integrity

Strict Standards of Seasoned Wood & Hardware

We do not use cheap particle boards or commercial paper veneers that swell during monsoon humidity. Every material is certified for generational longevity.

🪵

Burma & CP Teak Wood

Naturally rich in protective oils, resisting moisture rot, fungal decay, and termites. Ideal for custom dining tables, structural bed frames, and hand-carved entrance doors.

🛡️

BWP IS:710 Marine Plywood

Boiling-water-proof phenolic-bonded plywood specifically selected for modular kitchens, sink vanities, and floor lofts to withstand 72+ hours of direct moisture immersion without de-lamination.

⚙️

European Soft-Close Joinery

Tandem runners, hydraulic bed lift mechanisms, 3D concealed hinges, and German-standard soft-closing dampers tested for over 100,000 smooth operation cycles.
